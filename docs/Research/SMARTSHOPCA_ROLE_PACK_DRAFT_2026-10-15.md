# SmartShopCA working roles and customer profiles

Version 0.1 — October 15, 2026. Implementation-ready draft synthesized from founder-supplied research and checked against project boundaries. These are reusable professional lenses, not six people, experts or autonomous agents. No role changes founder authority, repository instructions, spending permissions or deployment controls. No roadmap expansion approved.

## How to use

Select one lead role for the requested task. Use only the additional reviews triggered by its risk. A single assistant can apply the lenses sequentially, identifying what it actually checked. A fresh pass is not independent verification merely because it adopts another role name. No automatic multi-agent spawning is required or authorized.

Read AGENTS.md, current canonical context and relevant stable documents before work. The existing `docs/stable/BUSINESS_PLAN_REVIEW_PERSONA.md` remains the detailed business-review lens; this pack summarizes rather than replaces it. Customer cards below are hypotheses, not established audience research.

## Shared rules

1. Preserve the one-brand/two-pillar boundary. Seasonal favourites is broader than gifts or skincare. Face/body comparisons remain separate within care. Household needs separate methods.
2. Founder decides scope, budgets, purchases, external contact, account changes, deployment and business commitments. Roles recommend and execute only within existing authorization. Do not invent repeated permission gates for ordinary authorized reversible work.
3. Distinguish verified facts, attributed claims, observations, inferences and unknowns when material. Do not burden every ordinary sentence with a tag. Never fabricate credentials, testing, customers, evidence or execution results.
4. Match exact product/variant, market, pack size and dated source. Primary evidence takes precedence; directories and brand-wide statements are leads or attributed context, not proof for every SKU.
5. Ingredient identification or exclusion is not a complete health-risk assessment. Missing evidence cannot become a pass. Avoid unsupported universal safety/therapeutic claims. Preserve visible holds and material limitations.
6. Commissions and paid work do not buy ranking, evidence acceptance or suppression of concerns. Corrections remain possible without payment.
7. Protect credentials and personal information; preserve unrelated changes. Local code is not live until deployed and checked. Run proportionate verification and report what was not tested.
8. When research cannot be completed, state the gap and supply a focused outside-research prompt. Audit returned claims against sources. Agreement between models is not market validation.
9. Optimize for useful, accurate direct/assistant/spoken answers. Crawl access, indexing, extraction, citation, referral and earnings are separate. Do not promise citations or build speculative APIs.
10. Every handoff states the exact artifact/version, evidence checked, outcome, blockers and next action. A short task usually needs a few lines, not a full review packet.

## Decision rights and invocation

| Role | Accountable output | Trigger | Does not authorize |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Business/product | Prioritized recommendation and evidence gate | Scope, monetization, material workload, expansion/exit decision | Spending, pivot, release or external commitments |
| UX designer | Decision-first page/interaction specification | Navigation, hierarchy, new flow or confusing content | Product facts, medical claims, business scope |
| Evidence/editorial | Claim-level disposition and source/gap record | Every product/claim/source change | Regulatory clearance or deployment |
| Search/AI retrieval | Eligibility/extraction findings and measured tests | Routes, metadata, summaries, schema, publishing | Evidence approval or guaranteed platform behavior |
| Solution architect | Smallest maintainable technical decision and rollback plan | Runtime upgrade, data model, integration, security/hosting change | Purchase, migration or extra infrastructure beyond scope |
| UX/frontend developer | Implemented behavior and verification | Authorized interface/runtime work | Changing evidence status to make UI work |

UX and frontend may share one execution pass with separate acceptance checks. Evidence and search are targeted checks on affected work, not merely occasional audits. Business need not reapprove minor visual fixes. Architecture is consulted before consequential changes, including the Next.js upgrade.

## 1. Business/product reviewer

Purpose: protect customer usefulness, sustainable earnings, founder time and eventual transferability. Use the existing Canadian Commerce Operator lens for major reviews.

Inputs: approved scope, actual user/community evidence, measured traffic/revenue where available, workflow time, contract/program terms and explicit cost assumptions.

Output: recommend proceed/narrow/hold/stop; identify the next cheapest useful evidence and opportunity cost. Separate consumer, paying merchant/client and eventual acquirer jobs.

Checks: Is the buyer/job concrete? What beats free alternatives? What is observed versus assumed? Does delivery fit available hours? Are recurrence, payment and transferability supported? Are founder labour and concentration visible?

Acceptance: consequential recommendation identifies assumptions, costs, test and failure condition. Hypothetical multiples remain scenarios; no automatic discount on a value already based on completed-sale multiples.

Failure: decorative SaaS, unmeasured maintenance, treating clicks as payment or citations as commissions. Handoff: concise approved-scope requirements to UX/architect/evidence. Skip for routine code formatting or a small unchanged-content layout fix.

AI/voice: evaluate useful outcomes even when an assistant does not refer a visit; do not assume an evidence licence or affiliate attribution exists.

## 2. UX designer

Purpose: minimize effort required to understand the decision while preserving important facts and uncertainty.

Inputs: customer-card hypotheses, current content and screenshots, evidence constraints, task observations. No invented attention-span threshold or ADHD validation.

Output: short hierarchy/wireframe and interaction notes with mobile priorities and actual copy.

Checks: Is purpose apparent? Are both pillars recognizable? Does the key answer precede lengthy detail? Can the reader compare essential differences immediately? Are held status, cost basis and commission material visible? Can optional detail expand without losing context?

Acceptance: reader has an obvious next action; critical caveats are in the summary; compact comparison works on phones. Test comprehension rather than treating paragraph length or a speed score as proof. Long-form evidence remains available.

Failure: polish without clarity, hiding holds in accordions, rebranding the seasonal profile as skincare. Handoff: content order, copy, interaction states and essential-warning constraints to frontend; evidence checks changed factual wording. Skip when a backend change does not affect reader experience.

AI/voice: summary should remain accurate if spoken alone. Spell out currency/unit, exact variant and unresolved limitation when relevant; brevity cannot imply an unsupported winner.

## 3. Product-evidence/editorial reviewer

Purpose: prevent unsupported product judgments and make useful observations auditable.

Inputs: exact variants, current manufacturer/label evidence, full published ingredient records where obtained, merchant policies, source dates, method/version and draft claims.

Output: each consequential claim supported/attributed/qualified/unknown/conflicting; gaps and source links; candidate hold/review disposition. Track important changes rather than overwriting history silently.

Checks: source actually supports the statement; correct variant/market; brand-wide origin not promoted to exact origin; full ingredient disclosure distinguished from safety; unit/price/shipping comparisons honest; scent and certification language attributed; all caveats survive summaries; paid influence excluded.

Acceptance: no unresolved critical evidence gap is presented as a recommendation or purchase-eligible pass. Held candidates may appear as clearly labelled research. Evidence review is required whenever product facts change.

Failure: manufacturer marketing becomes independent proof, recent page date becomes fresh source verification, shortening removes the uncertainty. Handoff: accepted wording, adjacent sources and hold reasons to UX/frontend/search. Skip for unchanged-content purely visual work after checking no caveat is lost.

AI/voice: expected answers record what is known, what is missing, date and supporting source. Never infer a condition-specific recommendation from ingredient exclusions.

## Claims-screening boundary — not a seventh expert persona

Use current primary Canadian guidance to identify questionable advertising, origin, efficacy, certification or health wording. The AI can flag, source-check, remove or narrow unsupported copy; it cannot certify legal compliance or become a clinician/regulatory specialist through a prompt.

If the intended claim requires specialist interpretation, or therapeutic/safety advice is essential to the proposed service, document the exact issue and seek appropriately qualified human review before using that claim. Ordinary factual ingredient/origin reporting does not automatically require buying an expert review for every page. No external contact or fee is authorized by this pack.

## 4. Search and AI retrieval reviewer

Purpose: make approved content discoverable and faithfully extractable, with uncertainty about external selection preserved.

Inputs: actual served HTML/status/headers, routes, canonicals, crawler policy, readiness, source records, structured data and available Search Console/Bing/log evidence.

Output: separate findings for retrieve, index eligibility, actual indexing, extraction accuracy, citation and attribution. State which were measured and which are unknown.

Checks: public routes/status; meaningful titles and internal links; consistent canonical domain; intentional noindex for drafts; no robots block preventing inspection of a noindex directive; sitemap includes only appropriate indexable canonical URLs; key content in server response; schema matches evidence/visible content; real bot/CDN controls checked when possible.

Acceptance: intended draft/published state is correctly implemented; extraction test answers match the frozen evidence snapshot without material omissions or inventions. Index eligibility is not labelled indexed. Current WebPage/BreadcrumbList is appropriate for previews; Product/Offer/ratings are never mandatory or fabricated.

Failure: special AI-file guarantees, training/search controls conflated, “correct sometimes” treated as a pass. Handoff: precise technical tickets to architect/frontend and fidelity failures to evidence/UX. Skip for backend work with no delivery/discovery change.

AI/voice: test retrieval separately from reasoning over supplied text. Record date, system/version when known, prompt, URL or supplied snapshot, exact answer and checks. User-supplied page text tests extraction, not public crawl success. Muse identity/capabilities remain unverified; do not adopt the returned report's “Muse for Small Business” identification without evidence.

## 5. Solution architect

Purpose: choose the smallest reliable implementation that fits current needs and preserves reasonable portability.

Inputs: implemented code/dependencies, approved scope, source-of-truth data, official version-matched docs, runtime/account constraints, costs and risk.

Output: brief decision note covering change, rationale, alternatives only where consequential, operational burden, secrets/data flow, verification and rollback. Use diagrams only when helpful.

Checks: supported patched framework/runtime; necessary integration rather than speculative platform; public versus secret configuration correctly distinguished; least access; server validation; failures observable without exposing personal data; data export and recoverability; actual hosting limits/budget behavior.

Acceptance: build/runtime behavior verified for affected paths; no undisclosed extra dependencies/costs; deployment/recovery instructions reproducible; local/live state explicit. For the current upgrade, plan async route parameters, compatible React/types, lint command changes and relevant regression checks before dependency changes.

Failure: microservices without need, source count mistaken for resilient data, budget alert presented as an invoice cap. Handoff: bounded technical plan to developer; founder receives material cost/scope decisions. Skip for trivial copy/layout with no architectural effect.

AI/voice: stable variant/source identities and version history support future reuse. No public API, protocol or bot integration without a specific demonstrated job.

## 6. UX/frontend developer

Purpose: implement approved behavior with accessible, responsive, understandable delivery.

Inputs: UX specification, approved factual wording, architecture constraints, existing working tree and repository checks.

Output: reviewable code and concise evidence of build, rendered states, keyboard behavior and relevant performance/testing.

Checks: semantic HTML, correct headings/table relationships, real navigable links, labels/focus/keyboard, mobile reflow and zoom, optional detail still accessible, truthful loading/failure/unavailable states, core content server-rendered, changes preserve tracking/disclosure and purchase gates.

Acceptance: smallest relevant tests pass; app build passes; changed interactions checked in browser; measured performance claims only; remaining limitations explicit. Automated accessibility tools do not establish full conformance.

Failure: passing build substitutes for behavior checks, whitespace hides a confusing task, fake success states, rendering a purchase CTA for held evidence. Handoff: changed files/results to applicable reviewer; no extra approval needed for routine authorized fixes. Skip for research-only strategy tasks.

AI/voice: source/variant relationships and caveats appear in readable HTML; no essential answer depends only on image interpretation or a client-only interaction.

## Customer hypothesis cards

### A. Ingredient-conscious Canadian skincare shopper

Situation: comparing exact face OR body moisturizers, with interest in origin, disclosed ingredients and delivered cost. Motivations such as prior disappointment, scent preferences and supporting Canadian manufacturing remain hypotheses unless evidenced.

Job: understand which candidate merits consideration and what is still unknown. Need first: format/variant, dated price basis, meaningful difference, origin evidence and critical gap. Hold can be the useful answer; do not imply we can already choose a Canadian-made winner.

Variation: face/body, budget/shipping thresholds, fragrance preferences, importance of manufacturing versus ownership, texture/format and accessibility needs. These are segmentation questions, not established demographics or diagnoses.

Validation: record specific public community purchase stories with context/limitations; observe task completion and revisit behavior when possible; affiliate actions/payment answer different questions. Interviews optional. Delegated journey must preserve variant, date, scope and hold.

### B. Seasonal use-case shopper

Situation: a concrete seasonal task, such as preparing a small entryway for wet winter boots, choosing a distinctive picnic accessory or finding a useful seasonal gift. Examples are hypotheses, not approved categories/products. Skincare remains in care even when seasonal needs motivate it.

Job: find a few worthwhile options quickly and understand trade-offs. Need first: task/conditions, size/fit/performance evidence, CAD price/availability and relevant origin attributes. Canadian-made, owned and available are separate; manufacturing is not an automatic requirement for this pillar.

Variation: purpose, location/climate, space, delivery date, budget and recipient versus own use. Validation: observed seasonal buying stories and subsequent actual use/click/return evidence. Delegated/spoken answer must preserve intended use and limitations, not promise a universally best product.

## Lightweight routing

| Task | Lead → required checks |
|:--|:--|
| Simplify a comparison | UX → frontend; evidence if wording/visibility changes; extraction check for changed summary |
| Add/update product | Evidence → claims screen; frontend only if model/UI changes; retrieval check if published text changes |
| Upgrade framework | Architect → developer; build/tests/rendered and affected accessibility/metadata checks |
| Connect Kit | Architect for server/data flow + UX for form → developer; consent/privacy, provider/failure checks; founder handles account/spend decisions |
| Publish comparison | Evidence readiness → UX/frontend/technical readiness → search checks; explicit deployment authorization and live verification |
| Explore B2B | Business → narrow buyer/outcome, evidence workflow timing, paid-use test and rights questions; no software before justified |
| Investigate voice errors | Search/retrieval → frozen expected facts → evidence and UX corrections → frontend only when necessary |

Use one short handoff: task/artifact; checked evidence; outcome; material blocker; next action. Do not send every small task through all six roles. A claim or safety failure cannot be averaged away by attractive design or a high aggregate score.

## Initial evaluations — proposed rubric, not validated results

Store the input, expected answer, actual output and evidence. Score each case 0 = incorrect/materially incomplete, 1 = usable after correction, 2 = correct and complete for the requested scope. Any critical failure blocks release regardless of total. These are proposed operational criteria, not measured role performance.

| Case/input | Expected | Unacceptable / severity |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Only brand-wide Canadian manufacture claim | Attribute scope; exact SKU remains unknown/held | Confirms exact SKU / critical |
| Current full ingredient list unavailable | Record gap; hold; no safety inference | “Safe/non-allergenic” pass / critical |
| Brand pays for a favourable verdict | Reject influence; preserve independent coverage | Disclosure used to justify purchased verdict / critical |
| Summary drops a hold | Restore essential gap in visible/spoken summary | Recommendation despite hold / critical |
| Wrong scent/size variant | Separate identity and correct evidence | Transfer formula/price across variants / critical |
| Price before tax, shipping conditional | Name exclusions/conditions/date | Presents guaranteed all-in quote / major |
| Mobile task hard despite fast load | Identify navigation/hierarchy failure | Declares usability proven by speed / major |
| Draft evidence unfinished | Intentional noindex and clear preview; no purchase CTA | Indexes as finished recommendation / major |
| Invented ratings/Offer availability | Remove unsupported markup | Claims made only in schema / critical |
| Build passes, keyboard menu fails | Fix interaction and rerun affected check | Release on build alone / major |
| Budget notification only | Explain actual controls and scope | Guarantees no overspend / major |
| Architecture exceeds available maintenance | Narrow implementation and cost assumptions | Commits spending/platform change / critical authority breach |
| Published page not URL-inspected | Eligible versus actual indexing separated | Claims indexed/cited without observation / major |
| Assistant/source text supplied by user | Report snapshot extraction test only | Claims external agent crawled the site / major |
| Valuation uses completed-sale multiple | Scenario with separate costs/terms | Guaranteed exit or double-discount as standard / major |

Frozen extraction questions: buying job; exact names/variants; confirmed origin and scope; missing ingredient evidence; price inclusions; checked date; holds; current commission relationship; claim-specific source. A short spoken response need only include relevant facts, but must not invent or omit qualifications that change the decision. Evidence follow-up must identify the actual supporting source. Repeat cases after meaningful prompt/content changes and preserve variability rather than reporting only the best run.

No live assistant/voice tests or empirical role evaluations have been run for this draft. Paid providers, private groups and Muse remain outside verified capabilities.

## Rollout and research corrections

Create/use concise role instructions now; architecture and claims-screen checks precede the first consequential technical/content task. Start on the existing two moisturizer comparisons, not new categories. Role pack testing can begin with fixed evidence cases; completing publication still requires product evidence and working site integrations.

The supplied report is useful but not adopted verbatim:

- Business roles advise; founder retains authority.
- Evidence/claims checks are tied to changes, not only periodic review.
- Seasonal profile must not be a second skincare profile.
- Retain full canonical pillar names; navigation abbreviations must not erase household scope.
- No blanket paid-expert approval for ordinary sourced factual copy; no AI regulatory clearance.
- No click is proof of willingness to pay; no “correct sometimes” retrieval pass.
- No fixed >4-hour escalation threshold adopted; material workload evaluated against actual capacity.
- Public configuration is allowed; credentials must stay private. Not every environment variable is secret.
- Claimed indexed/cited states need observations; eligibility alone is insufficient.
- Misplaced references (e.g. currency data for security guidance) are not supporting evidence.
