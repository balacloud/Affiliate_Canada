Methodology and next checks

What was checked?

Manufacturer product pages, origin statements, disclosed ingredients and merchant terms, accessed October 14, 2026. Currency and unit-price arithmetic were checked. The underlying research records conflicts and access limits. No physical samples or checkout tests were performed.

What does ingredient screening mean here?

Working method v0.1 records disclosure and preference-relevant details. It does not establish comprehensive safety, endocrine effects, individual allergy tolerance, certification status or efficacy. Missing evidence remains unknown. The current physical label may differ from web content.

What has to happen before publication?

Resolve product origin/formula/stock gaps, define the specific buying question and review claims. Then complete the repository pre-publish checklist and repair website launch blockers. The two-candidate groups are draft comparisons, not approved product collections.
