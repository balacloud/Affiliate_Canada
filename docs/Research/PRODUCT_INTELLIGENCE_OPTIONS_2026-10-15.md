# SmartShopCA product-intelligence options

October 15, 2026. Exploration requested by founder; no B2B product, pricing, outreach, spending, roadmap expansion or publication approved. Reviewed using the Canadian Commerce Operator composite lens in `docs/stable/BUSINESS_PLAN_REVIEW_PERSONA.md`.

## Verdict

Proceed with narrow discovery and reusable evidence records. Keep consumer affiliate launch as current delivery. A B2B service could grow from the same research operations, but consumer readership does not validate business willingness to pay. Upgrade/hosting supports reliable delivery; it does not establish customer value or recurring revenue.

## Candidate jobs — hypotheses

| Buyer | Decision/output | Required proof |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Small Canadian skincare retailer | Select products using comparable exact-SKU origin, published ingredients and landed-cost evidence | Merchandiser uses a sourced report in an actual assortment decision and pays for it |
| Canadian skincare brand / Shopify merchant | Find contradictions or missing origin/formula/variant details across brand and retailer pages | Brand fixes identified gaps, values repeat monitoring and pays for subsequent delivery |
| Affiliate publisher | Track program terms, approved destinations and stale product/link evidence | Publisher saves measurable work and renews a paid monitoring service |

“Shopify merchants” and “Shopify itself” are different buyers. The latter is not an evidenced initial customer or channel commitment.

## What exists versus what is missing

Existing local application has typed product variants, dated source references, explicit origin/ingredient gaps and separate face/body comparisons. These are reusable foundations, not a proprietary verified dataset or established moat.

Missing: completed product reviews, measured consumer demand, durable event history/reporting, consented merchant conversion/commission attribution, B2B buyer requests, paid trials, recurring maintenance economics, redistribution rights and a reliable refresh workflow.

Product intelligence concerns product facts, discrepancies, changes and buying decisions. Affiliate intelligence concerns program eligibility, destinations, commissions, attribution and economics. Click counts alone cannot establish sales, profitability or incrementality.

## Competitive evidence

- [Shopify Catalog and discovery](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products): product data already powers Shopify discovery surfaces. “We structure product data for AI” is not sufficient differentiation.
- [Salsify](https://www.salsify.com/pxm): established product information, validation/workflow and syndication offering. This establishes alternatives, not the viability of our Canadian niche.
- [Shopify price-monitoring listing](https://apps.shopify.com/pricing-intelligence-2): generic competitor-price tracking is already offered. Listing claims are provider claims; performance not tested.

Potential narrower contribution: source-linked Canadian-origin evidence, exact variant/formula discrepancy history and actionable corrections. Whether anyone pays remains unknown. AI may extract fields, compare versions and draft alerts; human review should control disputed facts and health-adjacent claims.

## Risks and economics

The main risks are a problem too small to pay for, cheap alternatives, expensive freshness checks, inaccurate identity matching and conflicts with independent editorial coverage. Customer-provided corrections should remain accessible without payment. Payment must never buy a favourable inclusion, screening result, ranking or removal of documented concerns.

Public visibility is not redistribution permission. Verify data rights, contractual access and permitted delivery before offering licensed datasets/API; avoid reselling identifiable community-user content.

Track contribution per delivery = fee minus research/review labour valued at a founder-selected hourly rate, tool/data fees, acquisition and support costs. No price, margin or revenue forecast established.

## Smallest proposed experiment

Prepare one manual sample for a defined retailer or brand buyer using a small exact-SKU set and one concrete decision. Use public business/community discussions to discover workflow problems, then seek direct buyer action/payment before building software; no contact authorized yet. Proposed gates: two independent paying pilots, evidence of another requested cycle, and maintainable delivery economics. These are suggested decision rules, not founder-approved targets or market facts.

If buyers only want an initial cleanup, a one-time research service may fit better than subscription software. If no paid use materializes, the records can still support the focused affiliate publication. Consumer saved comparisons/alerts/shopping assistants remain separate hypotheses. No dashboard, chatbot, API, Shopify app, subscriptions or third editorial pillar is required now.
