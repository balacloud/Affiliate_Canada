### A personal question, a research-led approach

I bring more than 18 years of experience in software engineering and technical delivery, including work with complex insurance and financial-data systems. My work has involved checking data integrity, reconciling conflicting information, translating requirements into clear decision rules, and building review and quality controls.

I also design and guide AI-assisted research and decision systems. The discipline behind that work shapes my approach to SmartShopCA: ask specific questions, compare sources, trace claims to evidence, and make uncertainty visible. AI can help organize research; it does not replace checking the sources or taking responsibility for the published result.

### One publication, two distinct areas

We're building a Canadian discovery publication with two distinct areas.

**Seasonal Canadian favourites** brings together small selections of useful or distinctive products for the current season. Each shortlist is intended to answer a particular buying question, with reasons an option may—or may not—fit.

**Canadian-made personal care & household** focuses on product origin and transparent ingredient information. We're starting with separate face and body moisturizer comparisons. Household categories, including laundry detergent and dishwashing liquid, are planned for later coverage with their own review criteria.

### What clearer labels mean to us

A label should help you understand what you're considering. Our standard is to show the disclosed ingredients, explain the criteria behind a comparison, identify sources and review dates, and make missing information visible.

We don't use “clean” as a universal safety certificate. Natural ingredients, short ingredient lists and “hypoallergenic” claims do not establish that a product suits everyone. Our content is informational and does not provide medical advice or guarantee that a product will avoid allergies or other health effects.

### What we mean by Canadian

Canadian-owned, manufactured in Canada, and available from a Canadian retailer describe different things. We intend to label those distinctions clearly and assess manufacturing claims for the specific product being discussed. A brand's nationality alone does not establish where every product is made.

### How a product earns a place

Our comparison criteria include fit for the buying question, the quality of available evidence, ingredient preferences where relevant, price in CAD, and practical details such as format, shipping and returns. A recommendation should explain its trade-offs, including who should consider another option.

Manufacturer statements and published ingredient lists are different from independent tests or hands-on experience. We will identify the evidence used and avoid presenting research as testing we haven't performed. When evidence is insufficient, a product may remain a research candidate instead of a recommendation.

### Commercial relationships

SmartShopCA is being prepared for affiliate partnerships. Those partnerships are not yet confirmed. If we later use affiliate links, we may receive a commission when you buy through them, and we will label those relationships clearly.

Our editorial standard is that commissions do not buy inclusion or favourable rankings. Suitable products may be considered even when they offer no affiliate program.

### Keep us accountable

Product information can change. Our standard is to show review dates, make uncertainty visible and provide a way to report outdated information or request a correction.
