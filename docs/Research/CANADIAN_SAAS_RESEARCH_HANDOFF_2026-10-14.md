## Findings and limits

- Neither opportunity report validates paid demand. Regulations, complaints, and incumbent prices alone do not establish a valuable underserved workflow.
- ChatGPT distinguishes hypotheses, contrary evidence, advertised features, missing currencies, and access limitations more carefully. Its proposed bookkeeping workflow still overlaps incumbents substantially.
- Gemini's high-confidence labels and low-maintenance estimates often exceed its supporting evidence. Tax, language, tenancy, health-billing and employment workflows require specialist accuracy and maintenance beyond basic form-building.
- Gemini's bibliography includes mismatched support: a marketing-automation directory for estate workflows, a Stripe-fees tax page for broader seller tax obligations, and an MTFX exchange-rate page described as a Bank of Canada benchmark. Recheck material claims against primary sources before relying on them.
- T5018 research must compare free CRA Web Forms and dedicated tax utilities, not only full accounting suites. Annual pricing should be modelled as annual revenue, with seasonal support costs.
- Bill 96 scanning cannot establish complete physical-package compliance from ordinary storefront photos. Apprentice exports/signatures require confirmation of acceptance for the chosen province/trade.
- Perplexity's useful prompt suggestions: say "up to ten" and explicitly separate Canadian evidence from global inference. These edits have not been applied. Do not automatically prioritize regulation or narrow to existing SmartShopCA interests; those would change the broad-discovery intent.
