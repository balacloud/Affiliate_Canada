## 1. Verdict

Recommend a bounded customer-and-delivery experiment before a platform commitment. Keep shopping-intelligence/SaaS as a conditional future option. Neither seasonal-first nor skincare-first has earned a full launch commitment.

The reviewed materials do not establish a reachable specific customer, superiority to that customer's current approach, supportable delivery economics, or a recurring job warranting subscription software. Missing evidence is not evidence of failure.

## 2. Strongest credible case

**Inference:** SmartShopCA could become a trusted curator for a particular Canadian audience: discovering worthwhile products, explaining trade-offs, and checking relevant details. The original promise of fantastic, distinctive Canadian products includes taste, discovery, gifting, and support for Canadian makers, which the data-heavy draft underplays.

A maintained evidence system could improve the experience and its operating efficiency. Recurring customer workflows might later justify software. A separately saleable dataset remains a hypothesis, not an established asset.

## 3. Controlling evidence gap

Can the founder reach a specific group of buyers and repeatedly improve their actual decisions at a supportable cost?

**Document finding:** The reviewed strategy materials do not supply observed SmartShopCA customer behaviour establishing demand, return use, or payment. This is not a claim that every possible customer record has been searched.

The three AI reports have different evidence foundations. The [Perplexity archive](Perplexity_AI_Commerce_Strategy_2027_2031.md) describes its source categories as a research plan, not already-consulted live citations. Cross-model agreement generates hypotheses; it is not market validation. Platform forecasts and financial scenarios have not been comprehensively fact-checked in this pass.

## 4. Potential fatal risks

No demonstrated fatal business flaw was found. Stop or materially narrow the affected model if evidence establishes that:

- customers obtain adequate outcomes elsewhere and do not act on SmartShopCA's contribution;
- acquisition, research, maintenance, and delivery cost cannot be supported by revenue;
- a data product depends on access or reuse rights that cannot be obtained; or
- consequential claims cannot be maintained accurately within available expertise and capacity.

Failure of consumer subscriptions would not automatically invalidate an affiliate publication or paid research service. Unknowns are not automatic stop conditions.

## 5. Unsupported assumptions, ranked

| Priority | Assumption | Evidence needed |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Reachable buyers have an unmet purchasing need | Observed journeys and actual recruitment results |
| 2 | SmartShopCA materially improves the purchase | Comparison with the buyer's existing method and documented outcomes |
| 3 | Revenue supports reliable delivery | Actual terms and timed acquisition, research, correction, and update work |
| 4 | Users return or pay repeatedly | Behaviour across the natural purchase/monitoring cycle |
| 5 | Seasonal is the best first category | Founder access, demand, workload, and merchant feasibility |
| 6 | Consumer research creates valuable B2B data | Named buyer, workflow, required fields, and paid interest |

A useful consumer catalogue does not automatically become a useful business dataset.

## 6. Contradictions and unresolved tensions

- **Changing customers:** Affiliate publishing, consumer subscriptions, professional software, and B2B data have different buyers and acquisition methods. They are options, not an inevitable sequence.
- **Universal versus optional gates:** Section 3 makes all hypotheses prerequisites, although a narrower business need not validate skincare, subscriptions, and B2B simultaneously.
- **Independence:** Disclosure needs consistent selection criteria, consideration of non-affiliate products, and separation of paid brand work from editorial judgment.
- **Verification versus scale:** More records can create more maintenance liability. Measure useful coverage and update cost.
- **Shopify overlap:** The claim of no direct competition is too absolute. Shopify describes conversational search available in Canada, saved collections, and price/stock notifications, alongside structured product information in its Catalog. These overlap with proposed features; their performance for SmartShopCA's target audience was not tested.

**Primary sources checked during the review:** [Shop customer experience](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/customer-experience) and [Shopify Catalog](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog). Source check date: 2026-10-13. Recheck material time-sensitive capabilities when making later decisions; these sources do not establish demand for SmartShopCA.

## 9. Smallest next experiment — proposal only

Recommend a two-week manual buying-decision pilot with eight actual upcoming buyers, subject to founder-approved cohort, time, and cash ceilings. One candidate is a Canadian-made gift within a stated budget and delivery deadline. Choose based on customer access, not ease of publishing content.

1. Recruit eight buyers; record recruitment source and time.
2. Observe their existing shortlist or method before presenting recommendations.
3. Research only enough products for useful three-option comparisons; reuse records when appropriate.
4. Record evidence that changed a decision, ruled out a bad fit, or reduced the work needed to choose.
5. Follow up on what happened, rather than presentation feedback alone.
6. Offer a clearly priced follow-up service where relevant; track actual payments separately from merchant purchases or affiliate revenue.

**Proposed learning heuristic, not a market-fit threshold:** At least three concrete attributable benefits among eight participants, plus credible economical reuse of the work, could justify another bounded test. No benefit suggests changing the cohort or proposition. No paid uptake leaves paid-service demand unsupported but does not alone disprove affiliate potential. Two weeks cannot establish subscription retention.

Nothing in this proposal authorizes spending, contacting participants, publishing, or claiming validation.
